How this works

Drop monthly PDF statements into FREELANCE/FINANCIALS/CC Statements on your computer and ask Claude to scan them — this tab can't read your files itself, only display what Claude has already imported. Claude reads each statement, categorizes every charge, flags likely business/billable expenses for your review below, and detects recurring subscriptions. Nothing gets added to your Expenses tab (and nothing here affects an invoice or your tax summary) until you click "Add as expense" on a specific charge.

App lock

A passcode screen so an accidental click or someone else at your computer can't casually open or edit this. This is a deterrent, not real security — since everything runs in your browser with no server, anyone who opens the browser's dev tools can still see the underlying data regardless of the passcode. It just stops casual/accidental access.
